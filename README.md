# Bright Coffee Shop — Sales & Operational Analysis

## 1. Overview
This practical activity analyzes transactional sales data for **Bright Coffee Shop** across three retail locations (**Lower Manhattan**, **Hell's Kitchen**, and **Astoria**). The primary objective is to apply Python control flow and data manipulation techniques to evaluate store-level revenue performance, categorize customer transaction sizes, identify peak trading hours for staffing optimization, track progress against cumulative revenue targets, and determine top-selling product categories.

### Key Business Questions Addressed:
* How do transactions break down into **High** (> R10), **Medium** (R5–R10), and **Low** (< R5) revenue tiers?
* What is the total revenue generated by each individual store location?
* Which hours of the day experience peak customer demand (> 3,000 transactions) requiring additional staff?
* In which month does cumulative revenue cross the **R150,000** business target?
* What is the top-performing product category across each store?

---

## 2. Process
To answer the business questions accurately and reproducibly, the analysis followed a structured data-processing workflow:

1. **Project Planning:** Mapped out the logical workflow and decision trees using a Miro flowchart before starting development.
2. **Data Ingestion & Cleaning:** Loaded the raw sales dataset (`149,116` records across `14` columns). Parsed `transaction_date` and `transaction_time` to extract the operating `hour` and `month`. Converted comma-formatted decimal strings in `unit_price` into clean numeric floats and calculated transaction `revenue` (`unit_price * transaction_qty`).
3. **Transaction Classification (`if / elif / else` & `.apply()`):** Built and tested conditional logic on single transactions before applying the `classify_revenue()` function across all 149,116 rows to create the `revenue_flag` column.
4. **Store & Hourly Aggregation (`for` Loops):** Iterated through unique `store_location` values to compute total revenue per store, and looped through a 24-hour cycle (`range(24)`) with conditional filtering to isolate peak hours exceeding 3,000 transactions.
5. **Cumulative Target Tracking (`while` Loop):** Accumulated monthly revenue sequentially starting from Month 1 until the running total exceeded the R150,000 target.
6. **Modular Store Reporting (`def` Function):** Created a reusable `store_report()` function with a zero-division guard to dynamically output total revenue, order counts, average order value, and the top-selling product category (`.idxmax()`) for any given store.

## 3. Summary of Insights
The analysis of all **149,116** transactions revealed the following actionable insights:

* **Transaction Size Distribution:** The vast majority of purchases are small-to-medium ticket items:
  * **Low (< R5):** `93,463` transactions (62.7%)
  * **Medium (R5 – R10):** `52,182` transactions (35.0%)
  * **High (> R10):** `3,471` transactions (2.3%)
* **Store Performance:** All three locations perform consistently, generating nearly R700,000 in combined revenue:
  * **Hell's Kitchen:** `R236,511.17` across `50,735` orders (Avg: `R4.66` per sale)
  * **Astoria:** `R232,243.91` across `50,599` orders (Avg: `R4.59` per sale)
  * **Lower Manhattan:** `R230,057.25` across `47,782` orders (Avg: `R4.81` per sale)
* **Peak Operating Hours:** High foot traffic occurs consistently across **14 operating hours** (from **06:00 through 19:00**). The busiest morning rush peaks between **08:00 and 10:00**, led by **10:00** (`18,545` transactions), **09:00** (`17,764` transactions), and **08:00** (`17,654` transactions).
* **Revenue Target Milestone:** Bright Coffee Shop crossed its **R150,000** cumulative target in **Month 2**, reaching a running total of **R157,823** (`R81,678` in Month 1 + `R76,145` in Month 2).
* **Top Product Category:** **Coffee** is the #1 revenue-generating product category across all three store locations.
